    How your schedule stays private
    
    
    
      
    No server. This is a static page. Your file or pasted text is read by JavaScript in this tab and the .ics is built here too.
    
      
    No network. The page's security policy (connect-src 'none') stops it from sending anything over the network, even by mistake.
    
      
    No sign-in here. You sign in only on the university's own page. This site never sees your password, cookies or session.
    
      
    Inert parsing. An uploaded page is opened as an inert document: its scripts don't run, its images and links aren't loaded, and it is never shown here. Only course codes, titles, CRNs, meeting times and rooms are read from it.
    
      
    Nothing kept. Your schedule isn't stored; closing the tab clears it. Only your theme and appearance templates are remembered in this browser.
    
      
    Your files. A saved schedule page contains your name and student number. Delete it once you're done, and sign out of MyOntarioTech.
